What did the changes improve?

They make the experiment more trustworthy and easier to inspect. This pilot gives us no reliable evidence that the additional part facts produce better designs.

Useful infrastructure

Enforced text-only generation, isolated workspaces, bounded feedback and budgets, exact input and output records, versioned part facts, and a frozen handoff to an independent evaluation session. These facilities passed their correctness checks and preserved all outcomes.

The generating model never received these images or a visual critique. All rendering happened after generation ended.

Design benefit remains unproven

Only 9 of 54 treatment sessions retrieved detailed facts. All 40 small calibration tasks passed, including control, so they hit a ceiling. A weak retrieval result cannot establish that the information itself is unhelpful.

All 42 submitted creative builds have construction warnings. A compiled model and a high visual score do not establish physical buildability.

The four information conditions

Every arm kept E, the reply example, task constraints, language and legacy count/error feedback. Shape and connector facts were available on demand; baseline footprints and heights remained in all arms. No shortcut assembly evidence was added.

How much confidence should we place in this?

There were 19 infrastructure failures and 11 model output-budget failures. The original archive preserves an earlier misclassification of one budget failure. All 30 remain unevaluated visually; none is silently discarded or given an invented score. The 42 submitted builds received 168 fixed-view renders and 84 reviewer requests, with no rendering or review failures.

This is one model family, six development subjects, up to three repetitions per condition, and one automated reviewer. Scores are exploratory. They are not directly comparable to previous studies with different scales. The review recorded recognition before revealing the brief; conditions and generated source were hidden throughout.

What is worth doing next?

Use harder calibration tasks, compare automatic fact presentation with retrieval, and test mechanical feedback independently. Broader model comparisons and independent human reviews would strengthen the evidence. The separate 36-case feedback study, expanded poses and new design guidance have not been run. E remains the default.
