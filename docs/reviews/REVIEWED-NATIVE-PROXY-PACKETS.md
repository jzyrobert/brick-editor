# Reviewed native proxy packet preparation

`prepareReviewedConvexRegions` prepares generated, source-reviewed part-local native regions before world colliders exist. It validates finite shared Float32 coordinates, indices and expanded child limits. Rank-three regions keep their supplied exact supporting facets; exact duplicate coordinate IDs are remapped. Rank-two regions retain every triangle in their fan, rank-one regions use segments, and rank-zero support remains at its actual point. The query world's allocation accounting includes explicit convex facets, triangles and segment vertices. Every native child must admit before the factory returns.

The factory is a preparation component. Its caller must first bind a finalized packet to actual captured geometry and account all children across active rigs, including fixed compounds. `reviewedGeometryDigest` hashes the exact occurrence-local triangle surface with SHA-256, independent of material order and winding. Changed coordinates or triangle multiplicity change the binding. A filename or official namespace alone is insufficient. No runtime packet data, physical-support guard removal or default friction change is included here.

Focused tests exercise explicit thin tetrahedron support with exact duplicate IDs, native direct rays, triangle/segment contacts, expanded fan-budget refusal before native allocation, malformed facet and non-Float32 refusal, and exact surface binding without source-buffer mutation. Native convex shape export is not used as a geometry oracle because the pinned wrapper can re-hull explicit point sets when exporting them.

The maintainer compiler `scripts/build-reviewed-native-regions.py --input <reviewed-source-region-manifest> --output <native-packet>` rounds shared absolute native coordinates once, selects a centered Sterbenz-admissible child position, and enumerates supporting planes using exact binary-rational integers. It computes every rank and outward fan, preserves the input source manifest, and records the input SHA-256, derivation version and actual native child count. Explicit source-reviewed plane classes are inputs; the compiler does not infer arbitrary mating support from geometry. Its input-byte, region, vertex and exact-plane enumeration limits bound maintainer work. This is the native realization stage of regeneration; source-semantic region construction and its finite precision review remain separate prerequisites. No reviewed input data is published by this preparation slice.

The subsequent source-bound runtime preparation includes lazy generated packets for `18940.dat` and `18942.dat`, with their Philo CC BY 4.0 attribution, complete pinned dependency hashes and exact canonical renderer-surface digests. `loadReviewedMechanicalProxies(sources)` validates captures asynchronously before WASM/world allocation; its synchronous consumer binds the source revision, occurrence frame and read-only capture identities. Missing or mismatched preflight refuses rather than using a filled housing or surface-only rack.

Preparation retains all 900 housing regions as 871 explicit convex polyhedra, 53 triangle fan children and three segments, and all 1,245 rack regions. Fixed and mobile housing use the same three source-reviewed Z-volume classes and composed part-local child poses. Fixed housing spends all 927 children from the shared 4,096 cap. The eight-part fixture spends 3,657 children with its frame fixed and 3,917 with its carrier mobile. These admission counts do not establish phone performance.

The localized guide allowance requires both bound surfaces, a reviewed source-feature guide match and a matching prismatic axis. It only pairs the housing regions wholly at source Z ≤ −10 or Z ≥ +10 with that specific rack. The housing's central material, end stop, upper guide faces, foreign assemblies and actor contacts keep their ordinary response. Before each native step, or for both kinematic sweep endpoints, the policy projects the entire rack envelope into the carrier part frame. More than 0.05 LDU of outer-cap excess or 0.002 of relative basis error restores response. The envelope check catches small angles whose long rack endpoint exceeds the corridor; it follows rotating mobile carriers. These explicit simulation tolerances do not claim strict native interior disjointness under arbitrary solver drift.

This preparation checkpoint preserves the structural physical-acceptance guard. Its focused tests establish binding, fixed/mobile native admission, source material/opening examples, all native support ranks, carrier-relative allowance negatives, responding core and foreign parts, source preservation and aggregate refusal. Full production forward/reverse, reflected load, stall/retry, back-drive, mobile reaction and browser acceptance are separate requirements before lifting the guard. Default friction, mass density and physical targets are unchanged.
