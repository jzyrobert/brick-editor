# LEGO piece counts by set style: summary

**Release-year scope:** 2020–2025  
**Sample:** 142 distinct sets across seven selected product families  
**Underlying analysis compiled:** 11 October 2026

## Main conclusion

**Subject and scale together are more informative than subject alone.** In this sample, building-family medians range from approximately **670 pieces for Architecture skylines** to **3,000 for modular buildings**. Single-character BrickHeadz have a median of **138 pieces**, but that is not an estimate for all brick-built figures.

This is a descriptive comparison of selected product families, **not a complete or representative classification of all modern LEGO sets**.

## Measured distributions

Counts refer to each **complete set**, including accessories, bases and additional models. “Middle 50%” is the interval between the 25th and 75th percentiles, not a confidence interval. Medians and quartiles are rounded to the nearest whole piece.

## Findings by style

### Buildings: distinguish skylines, landmarks and modular buildings

**Architecture skylines** are relatively compact and tightly grouped: the four observed sets contain **547–827 pieces**, with a median of **669**.

**Architecture landmarks** vary much more. The sample ranges from the **1,476-piece Great Pyramid of Giza** to the **4,383-piece Notre-Dame Cathedral**, with a median of **2,022**. A single typical size conceals a substantial spread within this family. [^architecture]

**Modular buildings** are consistently large. Their middle half falls between approximately **2,905 and 3,216 pieces**, and the full range extends from **Bookshop at 2,504** to **Natural History Museum at 4,014**. Their median is approximately **4.5 times** the skyline median. [^modular]

For comparable projects, use separate reference classes for a microscale skyline, an individual landmark and a detailed modular streetscape building. Do not apply these modular-building statistics to ordinary houses, shops or children's building playsets, which are not represented here.

### Figures: separate single characters, multi-model packs and large sculptures

**Single-character BrickHeadz** centre around **140 pieces**, with the middle half between **112 and 160**. **Multi-model BrickHeadz packs** have a median of **265 pieces**, but extend to **732**. These are counts per pack, not per figure; pooling the groups confuses model size with the number of supplied models. [^brickheadz]

BrickHeadz is not representative of every brick-built character. Outside this sample, LEGO's **Spider-Man Figure (76226)** contains **258 pieces**, while **The Mighty Bowser (71411)** contains **2,807 pieces** for the complete set. These are illustrative examples, not a measured distribution for articulated figures or large sculptures. [^figure-examples]

A broader classification should distinguish small stylized characters, articulated action figures and large display sculptures, while separately recording the number of principal models per box.

### Vehicles: the measured family is display-oriented

The **Icons Vehicles** subset has a median of **1,241 pieces**, with its middle half between **960 and 1,506**. Roughly **1,200 pieces** is therefore a useful centre for this particular family, not for LEGO vehicles generally. [^vehicles]

City vehicles, Speed Champions, Technic and franchise vehicles catalogued in other subthemes are outside this sample.

### Botanicals: an intermediate size with a different distribution

The selected botanical display models have a median of **749 pieces**, a middle-half interval of **424–817**, and a full range of **217–1,194**. Their mean is approximately **681**, below their median. This illustrates why a distribution pooled across all LEGO sets should not be assumed to describe every individual style. [^botanicals]

This subset is restricted to five-digit set numbers beginning with **10**, combining the former Icons Botanical Collection and the Botanicals theme. Small flower packs, promotional builds and bundles are excluded.

## Methodology and limitations

**Source and counting convention.** The underlying dataset records piece counts and catalogue years transcribed from BrickEconomy's public listings, consulted on 11 October 2026. It uses the catalogue's **Pieces** field, not a sum of a parts inventory or a count of unique part designs. Catalogue years are not independently reconstructed global first-sale dates. This Markdown export summarizes the existing dataset; it is not a fresh catalogue download.

**Weighting and statistics.** Each distinct set number receives equal weight, regardless of sales or popularity. Reissues and colour variants with separate set numbers count separately. Quartiles use linear interpolation; displayed values use half-up rounding. Statistics were checked against the supplied 142-row dataset when preparing this export.

**Family boundaries.** The building and vehicle groups follow their named catalogue subthemes, not every model depicting the same subject. The modular group includes earlier Creator Expert products now catalogued under Icons. The skyline group includes the separately numbered Shanghai reissue. The botanical subset uses the additional 10xxx restriction described above.

**BrickHeadz boundaries.** The 89 included BrickHeadz sets comprise 31 single-character/animal sets and 58 multi-model packs. Packs include pet pairs/families and robot-and-vehicle packs with two simultaneous builds. Promotional entries 40412, 6322719, 40726 and 40728 are excluded, as are customizable or alternative-character kits 40383, 40384, 40541, 40542 and 40748. Ordinary minifigures and large character sculptures outside BrickHeadz are not included.

**Coverage and uncertainty.** Ordinary City/Friends building playsets, small vehicles, mechs, spaceships, animals outside these families and mixed scenes have not been comprehensively classified. The small cohorts—especially four skylines and six modular buildings—limit generalization to future releases. These are observed family distributions, not universal estimates for all buildings, figures or vehicles.

**Correction to the earlier discussion.** Previously quoted overall-catalogue mean and median estimates were not adequately verified and are not used in this analysis.

## Practical reference points

For the measured families, central estimates are approximately **140 pieces for a single BrickHeadz figure; 265 for a multi-model BrickHeadz pack; 670 for a skyline; 750 for a botanical display model; 1,240 for an Icons display vehicle; 2,020 for an Architecture landmark; and 3,000 for a modular building**. Use the table's middle-half intervals alongside these medians rather than treating the medians as fixed targets.
