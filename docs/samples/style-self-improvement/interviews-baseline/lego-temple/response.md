I would make three small process edits. First, clarify that intentional brick sculpting and shaped-part surfaces are equally valid treatments. That could encourage stronger stylistic choices, but could also excuse crude approximation.

Second, consolidate the repeated permissions for independent assemblies into one statement that also asks for deliberate hierarchy and relationships. That could reduce fragmented compositions, but might discourage useful separate modules.

Third, include existing warning records in each text-only check result. That would expose connection concerns without adding stages or a solver, but imperfect warnings could distract designers or discourage legitimate hanging assemblies. These are hypotheses worth testing, not established improvements.
