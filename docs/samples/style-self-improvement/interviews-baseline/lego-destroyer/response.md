I recommend three small process edits:

1. Clarify that intentional faceting and stepped construction are valid when they serve the form. This could reduce indiscriminate smoothing, but could also excuse crude approximations.
2. Include all warning categories and their source locations in existing checks. This would expose issues before submission, but could distract designers with benign warnings.
3. Add concise taper-direction and attachment information to text-only part results where available. This would improve spatial planning, but increase catalogue verbosity and reduce some uncertainty that currently tests reasoning.
