Sol 6.1 · Style improvement loops

All builds use high effort and independent clean starts. Generations receive the original brief, part target and text-only search/compiler feedback. Interviews happen after generation in their original sessions; interview text, critiques and renders never enter a new generation.

Compare silhouette, intentional surface treatment, style composition, visible support and consistency across views. Stepped/pixel-like construction and purposeful studs are valid. Scores are subjective single-sample assessments; compiler acceptance does not prove physical buildability.
