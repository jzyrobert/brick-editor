Retained leaf introduction prerequisites and actual availability are different obligations. A child leaf introduced on an unjoined sibling is not available as a parent receiver. An outside addition whose known recipient lies in a child must wait for that child’s placement, not just its introduction. New source-association edges can target joins; original support/host/access prerequisites must not be silently relabelled join-only or removed to permit a source candidate.
