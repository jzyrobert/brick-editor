The v15 deterministic generator separates a collared-pin joint into a held

brick, then a zero-new-part mount into the vehicle. It also separates the two

## Why ordering changed

The previous Crane draft positioned both receiving bodies before adding the
pins. Pinned 3673 geometry has two 20 LDU halves, a central radius-8 collar and
terminal lips. The recipients have nominal radius-6 interior bores and wider
mouth recesses. A straight outside insertion after positioning both bodies would
send the collar through a narrower bore. Final-pose association therefore does
not establish constructive assembly order.

Preparing the pin at the arm mouth before adding the corresponding brick keeps
the collar at the intended boundary and exposes its other half. A flat reorder
would still ask a brick to engage a horizontal pin while seating vertically on
existing vehicle studs. The separate held workbench postpones that simultaneous
mounting question to an explicit operation. Snap-lip deformation, retention,
support and the eventual mounting motion remain unverified.

The candidate retains all six original external source support/host members,
including the cab panel near the long arm tip. That cab relationship is an
estimated body-bound support, not a verified socket. Every original prerequisite
is retained. A candidate is rejected if its contraction creates an outside path
back into its members, even when the individual leaf graph is acyclic.
