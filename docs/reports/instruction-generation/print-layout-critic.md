## Actual baseline and defect

The accepted full PDFs contain Truck 77 pages for 57 operations, Crane 115 pages for 85 operations and Pizzeria 137 pages for 96 operations: 329 pages in total. Their exact-file hashes are recorded in the accepted publication summary (local artifact). The implementer's full text triage identifies 32 notes-continuation pages with fewer than 200 body characters: eight Truck, ten Crane and fourteen Pizzeria pages. This is a text triage, not a visual assessment of every page.

I reopened twelve actual baseline main pages: Truck 12/16/20/51/57, Crane 32/36/70/79 and Pizzeria 16/74/93. They include shutter and steering receivers, controls' surrounding source context, multi-lot steps, CAD blockers, conditional figure assembly, a zero-new-parts scene operation and the repaired accessory grips. Their pictures are useful, but fixed vertical blocks create unnecessary empty bands. The previously reviewed Pizzeria continuation pages 105/131 contain only one or two legend lines. A qualifier such as “fit unverified” is also separated from its receiver label in the text triage. These are concrete reading and print-economy defects, rather than a justification to delete cautions or shrink every picture.

I read the initial publisher at `b27d871`. It fixes the enhanced main-image baseline at 400 points, supporting images at 270 and notes at 241 even when particular blocks are absent. It subsequently caps the legend start at 160 points with `Math.min(y,160)`, including after a parts continuation has reset the cursor to 750. That last cap can waste most of a newly created page. Measured block placement should account for actual image, caption, action, tray and legend heights, including whether a supporting row exists.

## Review gates for the proposed repair

The completed prepared plan attached to each revised PDF must be deeply equal to the accepted plan, including complete source inventory, introductions, targets, receiver masks, operation captions, camera and CAD results. All action prose, supplied-assembly conditions, omission/access labels, marker explanations, part names/colours/refs and uncertainty statements must remain in printed text. Normalized text comparison may remove page numbers and continuation headings; it must not silently remove repeated safety instructions or qualifiers. JSON equality alone cannot prove that the booklet actually prints them.

The original main PNGs, supporting pictures and tray images must retain their pixels, correct operation/lot bindings and aspect ratio. Cached original render evidence is acceptable when exact hashes and dimensions bind it; a layout-only composition is not a new full GPU rendering study. At the same source capture dimensions, the new main image should retain at least its original drawn scale, supporting images at least the existing 120 × 90 point footprint, and tray pictures the existing 48 × 48 point footprint. Keep the existing readable font sizes: 11-point action/legend text, 10-point operation and lot names, with the existing smaller support captions and source refs. Preserve margins and separation from the footer. Fewer sheets obtained through clipped content, smaller type, distorted pictures or reduced receiver legibility fail this gate.

Keep the CAD outcome near the main picture, and associate the action caption, true-before receiver caption and full action instructions with that operation. Place each short marker explanation as a unit when it fits, so a final uncertainty qualifier does not become a separate page. Where complete text and minimum-size pictures genuinely cannot fit, an explicit continuation is acceptable. It must identify the same operation, retain the complete explanation and not imply that a new part or motion was introduced. A long action paragraph must remain reachable before the builder performs its placement. A zero-new scene step must retain its “No new parts” statement.

The final review should compare full metadata, full per-operation text and image identities for all three complete PDFs, then visually compare representative pages covering the former sparse legends, one/two/three supporting views, multiple lots, long operation captions, supplied figures, CAD crossings, scene joins and no-support steps. Adversarial examples should cover long unbroken identifiers and narrow/wide image aspect ratios; they must use honest overflow rather than hide or shrink data. Counts of eliminated continuation pages are useful supporting evidence, not the usability verdict by themselves.
