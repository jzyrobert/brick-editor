## Evidence and rubric

I reviewed all sixteen final model galleries: nine repository samples and seven official-set representations, containing **256 selected main states** with context, alternate and incoming insets. This includes the two additional Lighthouse states 59–60. Original PNGs/HTML are in `.local/instruction-round5/renders`, `.local/instruction-round5-official/renders` and `.local/instruction-round5-extra/renders`; the linked round 5 evaluation (local artifact) and sheets preserve this final evidence. This is sampled inspection, not review of every generated page. Oversized Hut sheets repeated a bottom strip in the browser capture; I used all four bounded paginated Hut sheets and its original exception PNGs instead. All four London pages, three Technic pages and two Shark pages were also inspected.

The rubric remains identification, placement/orientation, coherent batches, task/view continuity, connected construction/separate assemblies and honest uncertainty. **1 requires major reconstruction; 2 substantial editing; 3 is a useful editable draft; 4 is a plausible independent hobbyist guide after limited review; 5 requires polished instructions supported by builder testing.** The [round 4 review](round-4-critic.md) records the prior failures and export/viewer inspection; this round does not independently revalidate those exports or screens.

An independent comparison found that all seventeen generated plans retain the same unique introduced occurrence-ID sets and source hashes as round 4, with no duplicate introduction. Plans in the sample and official capture directories are identical. This establishes source/inventory preservation, not a feasible construction process. Galaxy is included in this plan check but has no scorable strict diagrams.

## Two rejected pilot regressions

The first pilot moved London tile `n233` before its only verified receiving contact `n228`. Body-centre heights differed by only 0.25 LDU, so the old height threshold did not establish their actual vertical seating prerequisite. The final implementation gives verified vertical antistud-on-stud seating precedence over that centre-height heuristic. The final London views put receiving part `n228` at 14 and tile `n233` at 89; the tile is visible on the existing support. Dependent lateral additions without a prior anchor are also separated instead of sharing an unordered picture. These final contacts still do not establish an insertion trajectory.

A second pilot made the House door `n8/n151` the final operation at 138, after roof join 137. Both actual views concealed the door, although its receiving frame was ready at 67. The broad top-approach rule treated a tall thin leaf as an overhead enclosure for other nearby work. The final blocker gate requires a body's Y extent to be no greater than both X and Z extents and excludes verified pin leaves. Receiving hinge hosts receive fitting priority. In the final House sequence (local artifact), frame `n8/n150` at 23 precedes a plainly visible red leaf at 24, while walls are open; the roof builds at 98–130 and joins at 131. The caption correctly leaves insertion/snapping unverified. Both rejected pilots are excluded from the scores below.

The vase below is partly occluded by the near wall, and physical fitting remains unverified. An open doorway in the source also means the old hidden diagram alone did not prove impossible insertion.

The estimated approach uses body bounds and a 12 LDU handling margin for parts with verified vertical underside connectors. It is narrower than an arbitrary enclosure test, but remains an approximation: it does not model cavities, fingers, grasping, swept insertion geometry or removal. Zero reported support/access conflicts means the bounded precedence calculation found no such conflict; it is not a clearance certificate. Same-parent and uncovered operations remain outside parts of this reasoning.

The source inspection also distinguishes lateral mates from vertical support. Hut `n252` supplies a horizontal stud to `n253`'s antistud; `n252` is not a missing female receiver. Its final states 148–149 show those additions separately. A final lateral male/female pair alone cannot determine which loose part a builder should hold first. Door leaf `n401` has two opposed pin/socket contacts with frame `n400`; final frame 94 precedes leaf 95, but snapping the two-pin leaf into the completed frame remains unknown. These distinctions avoid turning a contact graph into an unsupported physical-action proof.

The supplemental Lighthouse sequence (local artifact) independently checks another affected door. Frame 58 precedes exact leaf `n10/n220` at 59; the dark-blue leaf is clearly visible in the open cottage, before window frame 60 and roof growth. The alternate view still looks through the baseplate, but the primary picture is useful. Its supplemental plan is identical to the frozen final plan; the score is unchanged. This verifies a second actual early fitting picture rather than extrapolating from the repaired House door.

## Diagnostics, limits and next priorities

Compared with round 4, Cafe grows from 170 to 180 steps, Hut 225 to 247 and London 225 to 235. Sample unanchored counts do not rise; Cafe falls 16→14, Hut 76→75 and London 117→109. These are useful diagnostics, not proof of support or physically executable action. London visibility-review flags rise 15→21 despite the lower unanchored count; the result does not justify a monotonic readability claim. Cafe and Hut flags fall 20→14 and 27→16, consistent with the repaired selected targets but leaving other exceptions.

London and Hut still exceed the 200-step publication limit. Galaxy's 188-step plan meets the plan-size gate but strict rendering refuses unresolved body colours; it is **unscored**. Town (6,083), Cathedral (11,817) and Harbour (6,966 rendering occurrences) remain **unscored** above the 5,000-occurrence generation budget. No score is inferred from generation success, changed counts or absent diagrams.

The sixth focused round should address actual mechanism tasks: Technic 8832 axle/bush/toggle operations 31–32, vehicle tyre/rim/holder batches and hinge fitting. A useful refinement should identify the receiving part and mating axis from real evidence, separate dependent actions, show a close view of the interface and preserve inventory/source poses. An approximate route should remain explicitly unverified; an arrow derived from an endpoint or final axis must not imply clearance, fastening depth or a proved assembly order. Recheck Cafe caps, Hut clip and the early doors after every operation refinement so that mechanism changes do not reintroduce enclosure failures.

Round 5 resolves the specific Cafe and Hut hidden additions and catches two meaningful pilot regressions before final scoring. The final outputs remain editable drafts for the simpler samples and substantially incomplete physical guides for the difficult official mechanisms.
