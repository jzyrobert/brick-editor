## Meaning of a satisfactory result

A useful editable guide should tell a hobbyist which parts to select, what small operation to perform, which existing interface receives them, and how to orient the work. Unverified force, support, hands, flexibility and alternate motions can remain explicit. A repeated warning does not replace a missing procedure.

The proposed coverage target is the nine samples and seven currently renderable official-set representations: all should become useful editable drafts, without any still requiring major reconstruction of ordinary assembly operations. Galaxy is the eighth official input; it is explicitly unsupported under strict unresolved-colour capture until source evidence fixes that problem. It must not silently receive a placeholder score or fabricated colours. The three oversized samples likewise retain measured, explained refusals. This coverage contract does not turn sixteen reviewed models into seventeen successful diagrams.

I would be satisfied with independently evidenced draft usability, broadly rubric 3 or better, across the renderable corpus, with critical remaining uncertainty expressed as localized review tasks. A rubric 4 judgment may follow better actual sequences and diagrams. Rubric 5 and physical-build certification require builder evidence and are outside this desk review. Scores follow actual sequence quality; increased step counts, fewer warnings, more CAD CLEAR results or newly added labels do not automatically earn an increase.

A localized review task must identify the affected part/module, known receiving body or competing candidates, the uncertain interface or action, and the decision needed to finish the plan. If the builder still has to research an entire unknown mechanism from final coordinates, that model remains below the useful-draft threshold. UNKNOWN itself is acceptable; unlocalized ambiguity is the usability defect.

## Highest-impact deterministic work

1. **Wheel, tyre and holder operations.** The actual Mobile Crane6361 first step and Mobile Police6450 first two steps mix tyre/rim/holder bodies in final installed poses. Car and Jeep show related failures. Use pinned narrow family profiles, unique axis/centre/span matches and exclusion of ambiguous or nonphysical candidates. Build a recognizable wheel unit, then locate its receiving holder/axle. If fitting a tyre around a rim is a candidate deformable operation, show it as such; do not invent a rigid insertion route or exempt all tyre/receiver surfaces. Multiple wheel units need unambiguous destination correspondence.
2. **Receiving interfaces and roll.** The Technic axle-before-collar repair is useful but does not identify receiving holes, cross-shaped roll or fastening. Extend profiles only to actual pinned source-reviewed interface features, with verified versus candidate evidence kept separate. The actual required-axle CAD crossings must remain truthful. One good side/interface detail and a named host can improve a reviewable operation without claiming compatibility. Generic coaxial proximity is insufficient to certify a mate.
3. **Build/place beyond the strict contact-only module gate.** Existing source sections are useful candidates, but strict complete-connector coverage excludes many objects that clearly need separate building in the LEGO booklets. Add separate, explicitly uncertain source/profile-based candidate evidence rather than weakening the verified contact graph. Validate ownership, dependency contraction and full replay before accepting each candidate. Prioritize figures, Hut bird/quad, train vehicles and separate track placement, and Shark head/jaw/tail. A disconnected cluster must not become one falsely coherent module merely because it fits a compact box.
4. **Work orientation and underside operations.** Train underside plates and Shark head/jaw operations need usable working views and, where applicable, an explicit proposed model-turn action with pre/post whole-assembly orientation. That proposal must be distinct from camera rotation. Show what to hold or retain when unsupported handling/loose parts make the turn uncertain. Avoid adding a flip icon to a camera change and calling the action validated.
5. **Complete unresolved-input guidance.** Flexible drawing owners retain their true source geometry but need an identity/length mapping decision to become a useful physical inventory. Unknown or ambiguous interface cases should be organized as specific editor review tasks. Source inventory or user assignment can supply evidence; guesses cannot. Galaxy's strict colour issue needs authored colour evidence or an honest refusal.
6. **Compact and responsive presentation.** Move nonrepeated general cautions to introductory/help content while keeping operation-specific uncertainty visible. Keep required-receiver crossing information on the main PDF page. Use responsive details/insets and reduce marker clutter. Worker/yielding/cancellable generation is useful because the baseline synchronous CAD run visibly pauses for seconds. London/Hut need a practical publication strategy—validated grouping or bounded chapters—rather than merely raising limits without measuring export cost.

These directions are still feasible to investigate. They do not require recovering a proprietary LEGO algorithm or proving all physical assembly motions. Failure of a narrow detector should produce a specific rejected/unknown case, rather than a claim that no deterministic improvement exists.

## Review protocol and stopping rule

For each substantive change, regenerate all seventeen supported source plans, compare source/inventory and unaffected programme/presentation, and capture actual consecutive windows for changed operations, receiving state, first/last module build and placement. Include full-corpus representative sheets so local fixes cannot hide regressions elsewhere. Check real-model publication as well as small fixtures; inspect final PDF/HTML output and responsive views. Report fresh versus reused evidence explicitly.

I will score the actual revised results and record which defects are repaired, retained or newly introduced. The comparison remains to the primary London/Hut/Shark booklet operation exemplars and source-authored OMR ordering, with the lack of a primary 8832 booklet stated. No physical testing will be manufactured.

A satisfactory stopping point meets the acceptance gates and useful-draft target on the supported renderable corpus, with all critical review tasks localized. If that cannot be achieved, a credible exhaustion record must identify each remaining defect, the concrete bounded deterministic approaches attempted, their actual rejection/evidence, and the missing source/interface/physical information preventing further progress. Mere implementation effort, a low warning count, a failed heuristic or a time estimate is not exhaustion. Such a stop should state the remaining limitations candidly rather than award unearned scores.
