# Development calibration witnesses v1

These are evaluator-only development fixtures, excluded from generation inputs and compiler namespace. They are public implementation evidence, not private held-out tasks. Two parts each, including a rotated slope transfer combination, all upright whole-grid Build Script v1 placements. Author: Brick Editor contributors; CC0 for authored arrangements. Original pinned LDraw geometry retains its library licence and headers.

World origins and bases were written directly from the LDraw definitions and unit arithmetic, not generated by `placementPoint` or the compiler. The 3039 top studs are at source x=±10,y=0,z=0: the high row is script z=1. At script level 3 the 3024 underside point meets one of these studs. The 3659 central opening admits the specified ground plate at x=1; lifting it to level 4 intersects the beam. The tile pair has planar body contact but no top stud. A 90°-turned 3001 has a 40 by 80 LDU footprint, and stacking two gives a stud-inclusive 52 LDU height.

Critical contour checks also read triangles from the pinned source independently (including subfile transforms), and compare emitted origins/bases to these literal witnesses. Perturbations exercise an absent stud row, an arch beam, coincident tile bodies and an incorrect stacking height. No witness is supplied to the generating channel. Missing connector coverage remains unknown; static contacts do not certify support, clutch or assembly sequence.

The legacy 3659 bounding envelope has max source Y=33.412 LDU; flattened pinned surface vertices have max Y=24 LDU. The new shape reference uses checked surface extents for the starter palette and labels other catalogue envelopes conservative. The existing surface checker misses a plate embedded in the hollow end-support arrangement at [0,2,0]; this case is a documented coverage limitation, not the released known-invalid beam fixture.
