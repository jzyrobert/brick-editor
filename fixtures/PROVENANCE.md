# Fixture provenance

All model arrangements in this directory are original synthetic fixtures created for this project and dedicated under CC0-1.0. They do not include downloaded third-party model designs. Referenced official part geometry has its own retained CC BY 4.0 notices under `public/libraries` and `public/notices/LDRAW.txt`.

- `ldraw/nested.mpd`: two instances of a shared room; inherited and fixed colour; root STEP; unknown metadata retention.
- `ldraw/custom.mpd`: original triangles, fixed yellow decoration, ordinary and conditional lines, BFC CCW. Its front faces negative LDraw Z; it is visual-only geometry, not a purchase-ready catalogue part.
- `ldraw/200-parts.mpd`: deterministic 20×10 grid of 3001 references, alternating red/white; expected 100 units per colour and 200 total.
- `ldraw/studio.mpd`: original 74-part open room/table arrangement, generated by `src/catalog/templates.ts` (the `room` test-fixture template; no longer offered in the chooser).
- `ldraw/wall.mpd`: original 40-part wall, generated by the same template service.
- `ldraw/mechanisms.mpd`: original CC0 hinged door/frame and planar car with original cylindrical wheels. `src/mechanisms/fixtures.ts` adds explicit rig definitions; no automatic mechanical inference or purchasing identity is claimed.
- `ldraw/exploration.mpd`: original CC0 room generated by `src/catalog/exploration.ts`; 48×96 LDU doorway, four 8 LDU steps, a low alcove, ceiling strip and floor, with no assumed marketplace identity.
- `ldraw/scoped-materials.mpd`: original CC0 nested local-colour/BFC scope fixture; `scoped-materials.expected.json` records independent expected semantic values.
- `ldraw/full-library.ldr`: original CC0 row of official parts outside the curated catalogue (doors 3861 via its moved-to redirect and 60616b, which joined the catalogue in release `catalogue-2026-09-29`, dome 86500, round-top cylinder 30367c, fences 6079 and 32932, arch 15254, shutter holder 3581 and shutter 3582) plus one 3001, resolved from the complete official library pack.
- `ldraw/finishes.mpd`: original CC0 111-part finishes showcase (light bluish grey plates, a red/white/blue/yellow wall, 1×1 columns, and a 2×2 row in Trans-Clear, Trans-Red, Chrome Silver/Gold, Pearl Gold, Metallic Silver, Rubber Black, Glitter Trans-Clear and Speckle Black-Silver) used for render-look screenshots and tests.
- `renders/interior.camera.json`: exact interior-position camera for the studio fixture.
- Additional security, scope, hidden-layer, override, reflection, missing-reference, cycle, malformed-token, XML-character and archive-corruption fixtures are intentionally constructed inline in the tests, so each is hand-auditable beside its expected result.

- `ldraw/textured-parts.ldr`: original CC0 row of four official `!TEXMAP` parts (tile 87079pxf, brick 39266p01, minifig head 3626cpx0, globe hemisphere 61287p01) used by the texture browser tests; their geometry and images come from the pinned complete pack and its texture pack.

No texture images of their own, character assets, LDCad shadow data, Minebench textures or proprietary instruction models are included. Broader texture, composite, printed/left/right part fixtures remain outstanding; see the status report.

- `ldraw-parts/door-frame-30179/`: unmodified official LDraw library files, not original fixtures: `parts/30179.dat` (Door 1 × 4 × 6 Frame Type 1; author Ildefonso Zanette; CC BY 2.0 and CC BY 4.0) and the four primitives it needs that the pinned catalogue pack lacks (`p/stud9.dat` Magnus Forsberg, `p/2-4ring1.dat` Chris Dee, `p/3-8ndis.dat` Alex Taylor, `p/9-16edge.dat` Orion Pobursky; CC BY 4.0). Retrieved 2026-09-28 from https://library.ldraw.org/library/official/ with their author, licence and history headers intact (see `public/notices/LDRAW.txt` for the library attribution); `tests/unit/connector-fits.test.ts` pins their SHA-256. They show that hinge sockets and door seats derive from a frame outside the catalogue; the frame is not placeable in the app.
- `ldraw/bfc-branches.mpd` and `ldraw/bfc-branches.expected.mpd`: original CC0 seven-case ancestor/internal-dependency BFC arrangement and manually expanded polygon reference. Front/back browser comparisons use an independent whole-MPD loader; local colours, reflected references, inversion and inherited NOCLIP are exercised.
- `ldraw/door-room.ldr`: original CC0 room (same text as the `door-room` test-fixture template, `src/catalog/door-room.ts`) that references official pinned-pack parts 60596 (Door 1 x 4 x 6 Frame), 60616a (Door 1 x 4 x 6 Smooth), 3008 and 3010 by name only. It copies no LDraw geometry; the parts come from the CC BY 4.0 library pack at load time.
- `src/play/door-parts.json` (hinge table): derived facts (axes, pivots, extents, exclusion reasons) computed by `scripts/build-door-table.ts` from the official LDraw complete library (CC BY 4.0; archive sha256 recorded in the file). No part geometry is redistributed by the table.
- `ldraw/templates/playground-park.mpd`: original CC0 sample build (Brick Editor contributors, 2026-09-30), generated by `src/catalog/builds/playground.ts` through `npm run templates` (preview `public/templates/playground.webp`). It references official parts by name only.
- Backdrop textures (sky, grass, street map, sand, sea, studio floor, horizon silhouettes) are drawn procedurally at runtime by `src/render/environment-textures.ts`; no image files are shipped for them.
- The physics playground test-fixture template (`physicsFixture`) is original CC0 geometry, like the mechanisms fixture.
- `ldraw/templates/house-with-garden.mpd`, `ldraw/templates/small-castle.mpd` and `ldraw/templates/roadster.mpd`: original CC0 template builds designed for this project (Brick Editor contributors, 2026-09-29), generated by `src/catalog/builds/{house,castle,car}.ts` through `npm run templates` (`scripts/build-templates.ts`), which also renders the chooser previews `public/templates/*.webp` with the app's own renderer. They reference official LDraw parts by name only and copy no LDraw geometry: the house uses only curated-pack parts; the castle adds Flag 2 × 2 (2335); the roadster adds Plate 2 × 2 with 2 Wheel Pins (4600), Wheel Rim 6.4 × 8 (4624), Tyre 6/50 × 8 Offset Tread (3641), Car Mudguard 2 × 4 (3788), Windscreen 2 × 4 × 2 (3823), Minifig Seat 2 × 2 (4079) and Car Steering Stand and Wheel (3829c01), all from the complete official library pack (CC BY 4.0). No existing set or fan model was copied. `tests/unit/template-builds.test.ts` keeps the committed files identical to the generators.
- `ldraw/templates/corner-cafe.mpd`, `ldraw/templates/windmill-farm.mpd`, `ldraw/templates/lighthouse.mpd` and `ldraw/templates/off-road-jeep.mpd`: original CC0 sample builds designed for this project (Brick Editor contributors, 2026-09-29), generated by `src/catalog/builds/{cafe,windmill,lighthouse,jeep}.ts` the same way (previews `public/templates/{cafe,windmill,lighthouse,jeep}.webp`). They reference official LDraw parts by name only and copy no LDraw geometry. Parts from the complete official library pack (CC BY 4.0): the café's Lamppost (723), Minifig Bicycle (4719c01), Brick 1 × 4 with "Café" Pattern (3010p20), Brick 1 × 2 × 5 with Cakes, Bread and Croissant Sticker (2454adfa) and Tile 2 × 2 with Coffee Cups Pattern (3068bp25); the farm's Animal Cow (64452p01, 64452p02), Animal Pig (87621p01) and Animal Chicken (95342); the lighthouse's Boat 5 × 14 × 2 (2551); the jeep's Windscreen 2 × 6 × 2 (4176), Car Mudguard 4 × 2.5 × 2 (50745), Wheel Rim 12 × 11 (6014b) and Tyre 12/61 × 11 Balloon (56890). Everything else is in the curated pack. No existing set or fan model was copied. `tests/unit/template-samples.test.ts` keeps the committed files identical to the generators.
- `tests/unit/library-update.test.ts` builds two tiny synthetic official-format archives inline (original CC0 geometry with LDraw-style headers) to exercise the complete-library update path; they are not LDraw library files.
- `src/catalog/color-availability.json` (and `src/catalog/colors.json`, `scripts/color-joins.json`): derived part/colour facts. Verified lists come from the reviewed BrickLink evidence (`scripts/bricklink-review.json`); derived lists from Rebrickable's database downloads (https://rebrickable.com/downloads/, retrieved 2026-09-30 including `part_relationships.csv` for mould-table candidates, hashes in `scripts/rebrickable-snapshot.json`; attribution in `public/notices/REBRICKABLE.txt`), joined to LDraw parts by the Rebrickable numbers the LDraw files state in their own keywords (CC BY 4.0) or by the hand-checked mould table `scripts/mould-joins.json` (original CC0 decisions); colour identities from LDConfig.ldr (CC BY 4.0) and BrickLink's public colour guide (`scripts/bricklink-colors.json`, retrieved 2026-09-29). No CSV file or catalogue page is redistributed.
- `src/catalog/mappings-derived.json`: BrickLink numbers stated by the official LDraw part files' own `!KEYWORDS` lines (LDraw.org Parts Library metadata, CC BY 4.0), extracted unmodified by `scripts/marketplace-mappings.ts`; `public/libraries/connectors-ldraw-full-*/`: connector and occupancy data derived from the complete official library's geometry (CC BY 4.0, same attribution).
- `scripts/mapping-review.json` and the `reviewed` section of `src/catalog/mappings.json`: original CC0 review decisions (Brick Editor contributors, 2026-09-30) over derived mappings, with evidence taken from the LDraw part files' own keywords and titles (CC BY 4.0) and Rebrickable's database downloads (part numbers and names; free with attribution, see `public/notices/REBRICKABLE.txt`). Nothing was fetched from BrickLink for this tier.
- `tests/browser/inventory-resolution.spec.ts` builds its three-part model inline (3001, 3816cpq1, 35371 by name only) and a synthetic retired complete-library lock (`ldraw-full-2026-01-01`, an invented hash) injected through the `__brickTestRetiredFullLocks` test seam; no such LDraw release exists.
